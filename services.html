Remove trapped dust and debris

Screen Cleaning

Dirty screens can affect the appearance of otherwise clean windows and allow dust to collect near the glass.

Screen cleaning removes loose dirt, pollen, dust, and common environmental buildup from suitable window screens.

  • Removal of loose dirt and pollen
  • Cleaning of suitable screen frames
  • Careful removal and replacement when appropriate
  • Available as an addition to window cleaning

Hard-Water Stain Treatment

Mineral deposits can leave cloudy spots or visible residue that ordinary window cleaning may not remove.

We can inspect suitable glass and recommend an appropriate treatment. Results depend on the severity, duration, and condition of the deposits and glass.

  • Evaluation of visible mineral deposits
  • Specialized cleaning methods when appropriate
  • Honest expectations before work begins
  • Protection recommendations for future buildup
