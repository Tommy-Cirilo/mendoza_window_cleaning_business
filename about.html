About our company

Professional service backed by personal commitment

We provide dependable window cleaning and exterior property-care services with careful work, clear communication, and respect for every property.

Our story

Built around dependable, detail-oriented service

Mendoza Window Cleaning was founded with a simple goal: provide customers with reliable service and visible, professional results.

We understand that inviting a service professional onto your property requires trust. That is why we focus on being responsive, arriving prepared, communicating clearly, and treating every home or business carefully.

From window and solar-panel cleaning to pressure washing, gutter care, and seasonal services, every project receives the same commitment to professionalism and attention to detail.

Our mission

Make every service experience clear, dependable, and professional

Our mission is to provide detail-oriented cleaning and property-care services while treating customers, team members, and properties with care and respect.

Reliability

We value dependable scheduling, timely communication, and following through on our commitments.

Respect

We treat customers, team members, homes, and businesses with professionalism and consideration.

Quality

We take the time to perform careful work and pay attention to the details that affect the final result.

Integrity

We communicate honestly about recommendations, limitations, scheduling, and expected results.

Our work standards

What customers can expect from us

Strong service is about more than the final appearance of a freshly cleaned surface.

  • Respectful behavior at homes and businesses
  • Care around landscaping, furniture, and work areas
  • Honest explanations of service options
